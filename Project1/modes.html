Game Modes

VALORANT has different ways to play. These are a few examples:

  • Unrated: a regular match without a competitive rank.
  • Competitive: ranked matches.
  • Deathmatch: a mode for practicing fights.
  • Spike Rush: a shorter match.

Game modes can change. Check the official website for current information.
