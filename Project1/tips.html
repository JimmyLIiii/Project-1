Beginner Tips

You do not need to learn everything at once. Start with a few simple habits:

  1. Listen for footsteps.
  2. Keep your crosshair near where opponents may appear.
  3. Tell your teammates what you see.
  4. Talk with your team about buying or saving credits.
  5. Practice and learn from each match.
