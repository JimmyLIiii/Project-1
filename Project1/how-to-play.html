How to Play

In the main game format, one team attacks and the other team defends. The attacking team tries to plant the Spike. The defending team tries to stop the plant or defuse the Spike.

  1. Choose an agent.
  2. Buy a weapon and abilities before the round starts.
  3. Work with your team to attack or defend.
  4. Win rounds to win the match.
